Catalyst

Upskilling marginalized individuals

Catalyst, focusing on skill-development with an extensive 4-6 week training program creating a cohort of 100 students from underprivileged backgrounds and conducting regular upskilling sessions for building their technical skills and essential general skills.

How it works?

Catalyst is a dynamic bootcamp that elevates skills in two phases, guiding you from basics to advanced concepts through comprehensive learning and hands-on projects.
